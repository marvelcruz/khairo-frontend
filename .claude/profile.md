## SWOT Summary

**Strengths:** sharp single-gender niche · clear packaged offer · strong social-proof claim (700+) · low-overhead online model.
**Weaknesses:** no website · unverified medical/credential claims · single contact channel (phone only) · small follower base · no public reviews.
**Opportunities:** a landing page + testimonials + lead form would lift conversion and credibility · email/WhatsApp funnel · before/after content · referral program.
**Threats:** crowded coaching market · trust friction from unverifiable claims · platform dependence (one IG ban = lost storefront).

---

## Recommended Next Steps

1. **Build a simple landing page** — program details, the 700+ results as testimonials, a lead form, and a click-to-WhatsApp button. Removes the "DM-only" friction and makes the medical-supervision claim look legitimate.
2. **Verify and display credentials** — name the supervising professional / qualification to back the "medically supervised" claim.
3. **Publish real client results** — before/after stories convert hardest in this niche.
4. **Add a second contact path** — email + form alongside WhatsApp to capture cold leads who won't DM.
