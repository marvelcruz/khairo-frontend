# KhairoDietClinic Growth Ideas

## Gym Partnership Ideas

- **Referral agreement**  
  Partner with women-focused gyms, boutique fitness studios, and personal trainers. They refer clients to KhairoDietClinic. In return, KhairoDietClinic gives them a commission or discount for their members.

- **White-label / co-branded program**  
  Offer KhairoDietClinic as an add-on program inside gyms. The gym sells it as part of membership or as a premium upgrade.

- **Trainer certification / affiliate program**  
  Let gym coaches and trainers become KhairoDietClinic affiliates. They get a unique link/code and earn per enrolled client.

- **On-site weigh-in / assessment days**  
  Host free KhairoDietClinic assessment days at partner gyms. Collect leads directly into the CRM.

- **Member-only discount**  
  Provide gym members a special KhairoDietClinic discount. Gym promotes it through email, WhatsApp, and posters.

- **Private Facebook/WhatsApp group collaboration**  
  Partner with gyms that already have active female fitness communities. KhairoDietClinic provides education and special offers to the group.

- **Gym staff referral bonus**  
  Give front-desk staff or trainers a cash reward for every gym member who joins KhairoDietClinic.

- **Free workshops / masterclasses**  
  Offer free nutrition, hormone, or weight-loss workshops at partner gyms. Collect leads at the event.

- **Trial week / challenge co-host**  
  Run a 7-day challenge with the gym. KhairoDietClinic provides the structure, gym provides the space and audience.

- **Corporate gym partnerships**  
  Partner with corporate gyms to offer KhairoDietClinic as an employee wellness benefit.

## Outreach Ideas

- Create a simple **gym partner lead list** in KhairoDietClinic CRM.
- Use source tags like `gym_partner` or `gym_lead` so you can track which gym referred each client.
- Automate follow-up tasks for gym outreach.
- Use WhatsApp/email templates tailored to gym owners.
- Add a **Partnerships** tab or future card on the Revenue Growth page.

## How to Use These Ideas

1. Turn on the **Referral Program** in Revenue Growth.
2. Ask partner gyms to share their unique KhairoDietClinic referral code.
3. Track referrals in CRM using tags.
4. Follow up with gym partners every 2 weeks.
